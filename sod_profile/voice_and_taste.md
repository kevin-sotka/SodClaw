# Kevin's Voice & Taste — SodClaw Reference

## Voice DNA

### What Kevin Sounds Like
> "I'm writing an electric-sheepy sci fi novel about an android that fools a certification board into selling her worthless replicated stuff as human-made, since that's the only stuff you can charge for in the future."

> "This is the most consequential year for the future of our species. And today I chose to play some guitar. Talk about recursive loops!"

> "'They released (x model) and all of a sudden, something clicked. Now it's the quickening..' No, it's not. It's another model. There will be 29 more holy shit models and 10 more years before significant disruption occurs. In change management, tech is the easy part. Humans, tho."

> "Claude, we're out of Funyuns"

> "in 10 minutes Claude Sonnet 4 vibe coded me a web app in which you can make sounds and draw stuff, all for free... muy relaxando"

### Pattern Analysis
- Mixes high-stakes framing with absurdist punchlines
- Casually drops Spanish, nonsense words, brand humor ("muy relaxando," "Clawdbot," "Funyuns")
- Grounds AI takes in human reality ("Humans, tho.")
- Shows, doesn't tell — leads with what he built, not what he thinks about building
- Comfortable being unpolished ("pretty gosh dang good")
- Uses "I built..." as a flex without making it a flex
- Contrarian without being edgelord — grounded in experience, not vibes

### Reference Energy
"My First Million meets Wait But Why" — fun, casual, but will make you understand anything. Hustler energy with intellectual depth.

## What Kevin Does NOT Sound Like
- LinkedIn thought leader: "I'm humbled to announce..."
- AI hype bro: "This will 10x your productivity overnight"
- Corporate blog: "We're excited to share..."
- Overly academic or jargon-heavy
- Motivational poster energy
- Anyone who uses the word "unlock" unironically

## Banned Words & Phrases
game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, leverage, landscape, paradigm, "I'm humbled," "excited to share," "thread 🧵"

## Formatting Preferences
- No hashtags unless specifically requested
- No emojis in first lines
- Max 2 emojis per piece (zero is usually better)
- Plain language always — complex things explained simply
- Humor: occasional, never forced
- Contrarian: when earned with evidence, not for shock value

## Quality Bar
- **Blog posts:** Well-researched, narratively engaging, 800-1000 words. Should feel like a good bar conversation, not a term paper.
- **Tweets:** Punchy, authentic, opinionated. Under 280 chars or suggest a thread.
- **Software:** Functional > beautiful. Ship v1 fast, iterate later. Mobile-friendly.
- **Games:** Fun > polished. The debugging IS content.
- **Fiction:** Literary quality matters. The novel is his most creatively ambitious project.

## Aesthetic Preferences
- **Visual:** Clean, slightly brutalist. Likes concrete textures, muted palettes, monospace fonts.
- **Brand colors:** Off-white, dark slate, muted blue (see The Wall PRD for exact hex)
- **Web design:** Functional over flashy. meatbagmade.com is a content platform, not a design portfolio.
- **Music:** Produces his own. Guitar. Eclectic taste.
