# Ideas Backlog

> The holding pen for new ideas. Per Kevin's decision rules: **new ideas go here, they do NOT get a folder, repo, or CLAUDE.md until Kevin explicitly promotes them.** The backlog being full is the point — it protects Kevin's ~1 hour/day from shiny-object syndrome.

This file was referenced by `CLAUDE.md` but didn't exist until 2026-05-23. It now does. SodClaw logs new ideas here instead of starting them, and the dashboard reads this file to show the Ideas lane.

---

## Format

Each idea is one line. Promote with intent, not impulse.

```
- [ ] <idea> — <one-line why> (logged YYYY-MM-DD)
```

When an idea is promoted to a real project, move it out of here and into `projects/portfolio.json` at the Exploratory tier.
