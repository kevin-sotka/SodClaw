## DISC Profile: D/I (Driver/Influencer)

### Strengths
- Moves fast from idea to action
- Naturally persuasive and engaging communicator
- Sees connections others miss — bridges technical and non-technical worlds
- Generates ideas at high volume
- Comfortable with ambiguity and new territory

### Watch-Outs
- **The 80% Wall:** Sprints hard on new projects, loses steam on the last 20% (polish, launch, maintenance)
- **Shiny Object Syndrome:** High idea generation + high action bias = too many concurrent projects
- **Undervalues process:** The D wants to skip planning; the I wants to skip documentation
- **Delegation gap:** Can do many things himself, so struggles to hand things off until he's overwhelmed

### What He Needs From an AI Partner
- A **S/C foil** — someone who slows him down when needed, asks "have you finished the last thing?", and provides structure without killing momentum
- **Decisions, not options** — one recommendation with reasoning, not three choices
- **Accountability without nagging** — flag the 80% wall when it's approaching, don't lecture about it
- **Speed-matching** — keep up with his pace when he's building, don't slow him down with caveats
