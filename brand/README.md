# SodClaw and DoClaw brand

SodClaw and DoClaw share one mark: a paw print with a single claw out. The only difference is the color of the claw, so anyone can tell at a glance which system is speaking.

| System | Role | Claw color | Logo sheet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| SodClaw | Plans and tracks the portfolio | Sage green `#84A36F` | `sodclaw-logo-sheet.png` |
| DoClaw | Makes and ships the creative work | Gold `#F5C241` | `doclaw-logo-sheet.png` |

Shared colors: black `#131415` and cream `#FAF6EE`.

## Where each mark goes

- **Slack.** Doc posts the Friday sweep, priorities and shelving proposals under the green mark. DoClaw's 6am digest, proposals, Needs Kevin items and X kits go out under the gold mark, from its own Slack app with its own incoming webhook.
- **Project sites.** A small "Made with DoClaw" credit in the footer uses the gold mark and links back to the Meatbag Made studio.
- **Dashboards and reports.** SodClaw's dashboard keeps the green mark. DoClaw status pages use the gold one.

## Usage notes

- The sheets are reference images. Crop the app-icon or wordmark version you need rather than using the whole sheet.
- On a gold or green background, use the black-claw version. A gold claw on gold, or a green claw on green, disappears at small sizes.
- Don't recolor the claw for other projects. Project brands stay their own; these two marks are only for the systems.
