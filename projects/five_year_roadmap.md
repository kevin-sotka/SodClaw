## The one-paragraph verdict

The portfolio isn't fifteen separate bets. It's one bet with fifteen expressions: **Kevin is the trusted guide to AI for normal humans, and he proves it by making real things in public.** The consulting arm is the business. Meatbag Made is the moat and the lead-gen. Everything else — blog, novel, games, newsletter, even SodClaw itself — is either content fuel for that engine or creative recharge that keeps the engine's operator sane. The highest-probability sustainable business is **AI enablement consulting**, and it's currently the *least* developed thing in the folder (15%, shelved, idle since March 2025). That's the gap this roadmap closes.

Five overlaps that matter:

1. **SodClaw IS the consulting demo.** "I built an AI orchestrator that runs my creative portfolio" is the single best sales artifact Kevin owns. The day job teaches ops teams to adopt AI; SodClaw is the show-don't-tell version. Nobody else walks into a pitch with this.

Finishing it isn't a detour from the business — it's the proof that "range is the evidence a real mind is at work" (POSITIONING.md). It's also the project scoring 5/5 personal satisfaction, which means it's the battery.
3. **Every build is a post.** Train blog, Gazette, Riventide, Roblox with the kid — the Content-First Principle already says it: the work generates the feed, the feed builds the audience, the audience warms the consulting pipeline. Content is the product *until clients are.*
4. **Insider credibility compounds across lanes.** "We know the machines, that's why we defend the hand" works as a brand stance AND a consulting pitch. Same sentence, two revenue paths.
5. **AI Company Trail is consulting curriculum in game form.** If it survives its existential evaluation, its job is teaching-tool/product for the consulting arm — not a fourth unrelated game.

Together: the brand makes Kevin findable and trusted; consulting converts trust to revenue; productized offerings (workshops, courses, templates) eventually break the hours-for-dollars ceiling.

## The five years

### Year 1 — 2026–27: Ship discipline + drive the stake
*Theme: stop building demos, start finishing. Nights-and-weekends, ~1 hr/day.*

- **Gazette issue #1 ships this NFL season** (already the open decision — hand-built Brief 3). Kill-date honored if it doesn't ship.
- **Train blog cadence:** posts 6–12 of 32 published. SEO compounds only with frequency.
- **Consulting un-shelved by Q4 2026:** name it (BRAND_ARCHITECTURE open decision), write the one-page offer (pick ONE — SodClaw rec: AI-readiness workshop for non-technical SMB teams), land **one paid pilot** — even $500 — sourced from existing network. The stake in the ground is a client, not a website.
- Novel: chapters 7–12. No systematizing. It's the campfire.
- Everything not on this list stays shelved. The backlog is full.

**Exit criteria:** 1 paid consulting engagement, 1 real Gazette issue, 10+ blog posts live, novel past halfway.

### Year 2 — 2027–28: First real revenue
*Theme: repeat the sale, sharpen the offer.*

- Consulting: 3–5 paid engagements. Kill what doesn't repeat; keep the one offer clients re-buy. Target ~$10–20k/yr side income.
- Revive **@sodtoshi / ghostwriter** — now there's a drumbeat of shipped work worth amplifying. Distribution finally has something to distribute.
- Each client engagement → one anonymized lesson post. The consulting flywheel and the content flywheel are the same wheel.
- Novel draft complete by mid-2027. Decide publish path (likely indie, under the mark).

**Exit criteria:** repeatable offer with 3+ clients, consulting revenue ≥ $15k/yr run-rate, novel drafted.

### Year 3 — 2028–29: Productize or plateau
*Theme: break the hours ceiling.*

- Turn the repeated workshop into a **product**: self-serve course, paid template kit, or cohort program. One, not three.
- AI Company Trail's verdict comes due: either it becomes the consulting arm's interactive teaching tool, or it's shelved without guilt.
- Novel published. The launch IS content for the brand ("two humans made this on purpose").
- Target: consulting + product ≥ $40–60k/yr. This is the year that decides Year 4.

### Year 4 — 2029–30: The full-time gate
*Theme: decision year.*

**Go-full-time trigger (write it down now, decide with math later):** 6 consecutive months where consulting + product revenue ≥ **60% of day-job take-home**, with ≥3 months of booked pipeline and 6 months of expenses banked. If hit → give notice; consulting becomes the day. If not hit → stay hybrid without shame: a $50k/yr side business on 1 hr/day is a win, not a failure.

- If GO: scale to workshops + retainers + product; Meatbag Made gets real publishing cadence with the recovered 40 hrs/week.
- If HOLD: optimize for joy-per-hour — keep the 2–3 highest-leverage claims, shelve the rest permanently.

### Year 5 — 2030–31: The house stands

- **Meatbag Made** = established mark: novel(s), music, software, blog archive at meatbagmade.com — five years of un-copyable range.
- **Consulting sibling** = the income: named, separate, productized, referral-driven.
- **SodClaw** = still backstage, now also the case study clients ask about.
- The kid's 10 or 11. Roblox Railways revives itself whenever he wants it to. That was always the point.
